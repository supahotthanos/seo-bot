• Faceless AI-clone/avatar video factories at volume (8-12/day × 50 platforms), 100+ AI social accounts on VMs/proxies — YouTube July-2026 inauthentic-content policy demonetizes mass/templated AI uploads (Jan-2026 purge: 4.7B views, $10M/yr); platform device-fingerprinting kills account networks.

=== OBSOLETE / HYPE — not fraud, just zero ROI (don't fund as a channel) ===
• llms.txt / "AI-only" markdown shadow pages for ranking — Google won't support it (Mueller = "keywords meta tag"); ~408 of 500M+ AI-bot visits fetched it. One static file max; real use is IDE/agent doc discovery, NOT med-spa AEO.
• FAQPage / HowTo schema for RICH RESULTS — FAQ removed May-7-2026, HowTo desktop Sept-2023. Keep markup only as comprehension; expect zero SERP feature. Don't build pages around it.
• Dataset schema / WebSite SearchAction (sitelinks searchbox) — Dataset only serves Dataset Search (irrelevant to a med-spa directory); SearchAction searchbox deprecated late-2024. Emit Organization, skip the searchbox payoff expectation.
• "Optimize for the o200k tokenizer" / logprobs as a lever — tokenizer is input-encoding downstream of retrieval; logprobs = measurement not lever. The only real win (tight dense prose) is just good writing.
• Cosmetic freshness — date-only "Updated 2026" with no body change; engines diff content delta and discount/distrust the lastmod signal sitewide. (Real updates = the white tactic.)
• 250-char keyword-stuffed title tags — Google truncates ~60 chars and rewrites ~60% of titles; front-load 60 clean chars and stop.
• Stock-photo backlink farming, dead-Meetup-group takeovers, Claude-Artifact/Grok-shared-chat "free hosting" ranking pages — low-relevance link schemes / third-party assets you don't own and can be deindexed any time. Marginal, decaying, not worth a bot's systematic effort.
• Bulk 1000+ low-quality directory submission (ListingBott-style) + mass NAP citation blasting — SpamBrain ignores them; adds NAP-inconsistency noise. Do ~20-50 quality citations, stop.
• Bing/Google Indexing-API abuse for general pages — Google Indexing API is JobPosting/BroadcastEvent only; off-label use gets quota revoked and does nothing. Use IndexNow (Bing) + sitemaps/internal-links (Google).
• Custom-GPT-in-store / "audit-to-LLM productization" / build-in-public flexing — marketing/funnel artifacts, not ranking/AEO levers; zero on-site optimization effect. Don't file as SEO work.
## What blocks a 100% SEO/AEO bot (the honest gaps)

WHAT STILL BLOCKS THE BOT FROM BEING A 100% SEO/AEO EXECUTOR (honest gaps — capability, data, and human/credential dependencies).

=== CAPABILITY GAPS (code the bot doesn't yet have — the real build debt) ===
1. NO PAGE GENERATOR. The bot audits, proposes, and applies FIXES, but it cannot GENERATE the directory's data-moat pages at scale. Every "programmatic" tactic in the registry is currently aspirational until content/programmatic.mjs (+ citylist/stats/comparison generators) exists. This is the #1 gap — the bot today optimizes pages a human built; it must build them from lib/data.ts.
2. NO CONTENT-DECAY → REFRESH LOOP. gsc.mjs + priority.mjs exist but nothing joins 90d-vs-prior decay into a refresh queue, so the highest-ROI maintenance lever (freshness on decaying pages) isn't executed.
3. NO TEMPLATE-LEVEL CWV ENFORCEMENT. image-seo.mjs handles images but LCP-element detection (fetchpriority/preload), INP 3p-JS deferral, and CLS slot-reservation aren't applied at the template, so CWV isn't actually fixed, only audited.
4. INDEX-DISCIPLINE IS PARTIAL. noindex-thin-tail / promote-on-data-threshold / pause-during-core-update logic isn't wired, so a programmatic build risks the sitewide scaled-content penalty it's supposed to avoid.
5. OFF-SITE IS A WORKLIST, NOT AN EXECUTOR. listicle placement, entity co-occurrence, and Reddit answers are drafted/tracked but require a human to actually post/place — by design (these are the manual tier), but it means ~30% of AEO citation surface (off-site = ~90% of citations originate off-domain) is human-gated.

=== DATA / MEASUREMENT GAPS ===
6. AI-VISIBILITY DATA IS NOISY + SAMPLED. LLM answers are non-deterministic and session-personalized; SoV needs N-sample averaging (built) but the panel is still small and the engines re-tune source weight in weeks (Reddit 60%→10% in 2 weeks), so the feedback loop is directional, not precise. GSC gen-AI report gives AI-surface IMPRESSIONS ONLY (no queries/clicks/CTR, not API-exportable) — Bing AI-Performance grounding queries are the only real AI-query data, and it's sampled.
7. CITATION ATTRIBUTION IS LEAKY. Most AI answers don't pass a clean referrer; only ChatGPT auto-tags utm_source=chatgpt.com (paid-tier, partial). GA4 lumps the rest. You cannot cleanly attribute which page won which citation — you infer it from crawl-to-cite joins, not measure it.
8. LOG ACCESS IS REQUIRED FOR THE BEST SIGNAL. crawl-to-cite and IP-verified bot classification need server access logs; on hosts without log export the crawled-vs-cited diagnosis degrades to inference.
9. KEYWORD/VOLUME DATA IS BUDGET-CONSTRAINED. Per the no-paid-API rule, volume comes from free Bing GetKeywordStats + GSC + intitle: proxies, not Ahrefs/DataForSEO — fine for triage, noisier for prioritization.

=== HUMAN / CREDENTIAL DEPENDENCIES (cannot be 100% autonomous by design or by law) ===
10. PUBLISH GATE IS HUMAN BY DESIGN. Every YMYL/medical/price page and any net-new page routes to one-click human approval (anti-scaled-content-abuse + medical liability). Full autonomy here would BE the deindex risk — so "100% autonomous publish" is intentionally not a goal.
11. GBP / REVIEWS ARE OFF-SITE + CREDENTIAL-GATED. GBP edits live on Google's property (new GCP projects sit at quota 0 until manual approval); review velocity needs real customers and is FTC-bounded (no gating/incentivizing/scripting). The bot can draft replies and track velocity, never farm or solicit non-compliantly.
12. ENTITY/WIKIDATA/WIKIPEDIA ARE MODERATED. Wikidata is community-revertible; Wikipedia has a hard notability bar most med-spas fail. The bot emits sameAs and can draft a Wikidata item, but cannot manufacture a Wikipedia entity on demand.
13. OFF-SITE PLACEMENT NEEDS RELATIONSHIPS. Digital PR, journalist sourcing, third-party listicle inclusion, and genuine community participation require human relationships/outreach the bot can only PREP (pitches, target lists, drafts), not close.
14. DNS/CMS WRITE ACCESS PER CLIENT. The "owns DNS+CMS" premise must actually be wired (Cloudflare token, GSC/GA4/GBP OAuth, CMS adapter) per client; until onboarded, the bot is read/audit-only for that property.

NET: the bot can be ~100% autonomous on the OWNED-SITE technical + on-page + schema + internal-link + measurement layers (white-auto), and a drafting/tracking executor on the off-site + content-publish + GBP layers (grey-manual/opt-in, human-or-credential-gated). The single biggest leap from "audits" to "DOES it" is shipping the data-moat page generator (P0). The remaining gap to literal 100% is not buildable — it's the human approval + legal + relationship + off-site surface that SHOULD stay human-gated.
